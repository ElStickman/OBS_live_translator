# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
personal attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and publishing another person's
private information are not tolerated.

Discuss technical decisions on their merits. Assume good intent while allowing
maintainers to enforce boundaries and keep discussions focused. Maintainers may
edit, hide, or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile.
Do not use a public issue to report private or sensitive matters.
